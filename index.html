How it works

Three steps. One better enquiry.

BuildPath gives a potential customer useful guidance before they are ready to call, then gives you the context needed to decide the right follow-up.

01 · DESCRIBE

They tell you about the job

A few plain-language questions capture the job type, approximate size, key details and photos.

02 · UNDERSTAND

They see a starting ballpark

The customer gets an immediate range based on your setup, clearly labelled as guidance rather than a quote.

03 · CONNECT

You receive a useful enquiry

If they want to continue, their details, scope, photos and the range they saw are sent directly to you.

The customer gets clarity. You get context. Final pricing stays with you.
