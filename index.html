Value on both sides

Useful for customers. Valuable for builders.

The tool improves the first step without trying to replace the conversation, site visit or proper quote that comes later.

For the customer

Understand the likely budget before calling.

  • Get a clear starting range within minutes
  • See whether the project may fit their budget
  • Add notes and photos so the builder understands the job
  • Choose whether to share their details and take the next step

For the builder

Start with a better-informed potential customer.

  • Qualify budget fit before spending time going back and forth
  • Receive project type, scope, notes and photos upfront
  • Know what range the customer has already seen
  • Give website visitors a professional next step, even when you are on site

The point is not to quote online. It is to give the customer a starting range that is useful enough to support a sensible first conversation.

Different jobs need different treatment. Unclear, complex or higher-risk work can be flagged for direct discussion instead of forcing an unreliable number.
