The story

Why I built it.

BuildPath started with a conversation with a builder mate in Taranaki. Margins were tight, the drives between jobs were long and too many site visits didn't match what was said on the phone.

That chat brought together the things I care about most: seeing good businesses do well and building practical tools with AI.

The goal is to help you win more of the right work. Customers get a ballpark off your own rates before they pick up the phone, so by the time you drive out you already know the job.
